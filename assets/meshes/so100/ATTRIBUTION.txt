Downloaded verbatim, unmodified, for use in HYDRA-UMC-STUDIO's
SoArm100Arm.tsx 3D view. Each real link's own URDF declares TWO <visual>
meshes (a body part + a matching "_Motor" part, e.g. Base.stl +
Base_Motor.stl) - only the primary body mesh is used here, the same
scope boundary this project already applies to gripper/finger detail on
other robots (see Toolhead.tsx).

IMPORTANT - this robot is 5-DOF for positioning, not 6: the source URDF
(Simulation/SO100/so100.urdf) has 5 real arm joints (shoulder_pan,
shoulder_lift, elbow_flex, wrist_flex, wrist_roll) plus a 6th joint that
is the GRIPPER JAW (open/close), not a wrist orientation axis - unlike
every other robot in this project's own j1..j6 convention. j6 is left
unused here rather than repurposed as a gripper control, to avoid a
silent one-off redefinition of what j6 means for just this robot; the
jaw's own open/close motion is out of scope, same as other robots'
gripper actuation.

Joint kinematic chain (origin xyz/rpy, axis) comes from that repository's
own Simulation/SO100/so100.urdf directly - see
src/examples/soArm100Kinematics.ts for where those exact numbers were
transcribed to. Uses the same "quaternion family" FK engine as Parol6/
Faze4/AR3/AR4/e.DO/M-710iC (arbitrary per-joint axes), truncated to 5
real joints instead of 6.
