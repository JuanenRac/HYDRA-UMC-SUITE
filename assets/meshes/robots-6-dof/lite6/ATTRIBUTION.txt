Downloaded verbatim, unmodified, for use in HYDRA-UMC-STUDIO's Lite6Arm.tsx
3D view.

Joint kinematic chain (origin xyz/rpy, axis) comes from that repository's
own xarm_description/urdf/lite6/lite6.urdf.xacro directly - see
src/examples/lite6Kinematics.ts for where those exact numbers were
transcribed to. Every Lite 6 joint rotates about its own local Z axis
(the xacro's own <axis xyz="0 0 1"/>, no exceptions, same convention as
the UR family), so the FK/IK engine is shared with the UR models - see
src/examples/urKinematicsShared.ts's own header comment.
